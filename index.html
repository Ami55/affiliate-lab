How prioritization works
For matched pages: gap = max(0, search clicks × target / 100 − affiliate clicks). Pages meeting your minimum search clicks are ranked by this gap. This is a scenario gap, not recoverable traffic or an AI score. Missing affiliate rows remain unknown; they are never treated as zero. Duplicate normalized page URLs are summed; fragments and trailing slashes are removed, while query parameters are kept. No URL is fetched.
